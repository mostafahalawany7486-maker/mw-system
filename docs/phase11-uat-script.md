# Phase 11 — Reports: User Acceptance Testing (UAT) Script

---

### UAT-1: Get a full picture from the analytics dashboard
**Steps:**
1. Go to Reports → Dashboard Analytics.

**Expected Result:** A single screen shows total properties, occupancy,
tenants, owners, active contracts, open maintenance requests, this
month's income/expense/profit, a 6-month trend chart, and the top
revenue-generating properties — everything a manager needs at a glance.
Pass ⬜ Fail ⬜

---

### UAT-2: Check financial health for a specific period
**Steps:**
1. Go to Reports → Financial. Set a date range covering the last quarter.

**Expected Result:** Total income, total expense, net cash flow,
outstanding balances (both tenant and owner side), and the rent
collection rate all display clearly, with a breakdown of transactions by
type.
Pass ⬜ Fail ⬜

---

### UAT-3: Find your best and worst performing properties
**Steps:**
1. Go to Reports → Profit. Review the "Profit by Property" table.

**Expected Result:** Properties are ranked by profit (income minus
expense) for the selected period, so it's immediately obvious which
properties are performing well and which are costing more than they earn.
Pass ⬜ Fail ⬜

---

### UAT-4: Check current vacancy and act on it
**Steps:**
1. Go to Reports → Vacancy.

**Expected Result:** A clear vacancy rate and a list of currently vacant
properties, sorted by how long they've sat empty — properties vacant
over 60 days are visually flagged, prompting action.
Pass ⬜ Fail ⬜

---

### UAT-5: Review occupancy trends over time
**Steps:**
1. Go to Reports → Occupancy.

**Expected Result:** Overall occupancy rate, a breakdown by property
type, and a 6-month trend so you can see whether occupancy is improving
or declining.
Pass ⬜ Fail ⬜

---

### UAT-6: Identify your top tenants and any collection risk
**Steps:**
1. Go to Reports → Tenants.

**Expected Result:** Top-paying tenants and tenants with outstanding
balances are both visible at a glance, alongside how many contracts are
expiring soon — useful for renewal outreach.
Pass ⬜ Fail ⬜

---

### UAT-7: Review owner portfolio and payables
**Steps:**
1. Go to Reports → Owners.

**Expected Result:** Owners are ranked by how many properties they have
under management, with any outstanding payables to them clearly listed.
Pass ⬜ Fail ⬜
